Lab 02 — Circle

This is a circle moving around

Notes

As the circle moves around and bounces off the wall, it changes its direction and size when it hits the wall. When the ball is on the left side, its color turns to light pink and right side to sky blue. When you click the canvas it will change the horizontal direction and change the color randomly. When you press “S” or “s”, it will stop, move to the centre, change its color to white.
